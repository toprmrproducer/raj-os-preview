How many companies does Shreyas Raj run?

Two. Shreyas Raj is the founder of RapidXAI, which builds AI voice agents, automation and websites for businesses, and of EducateX AI, which teaches practical AI skills. He also runs a YouTube channel, AI with Shreyas Raj, and publishes a set of public projects. He is based in Pune, India.

RapidXAI

RapidXAI is the client-facing company. It builds AI voice agents that answer and place phone calls, WhatsApp and workflow automation, and conversion-focused websites. It also offers AI engine optimization (AEO), which makes a business easier for ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude to find and recommend. Clients are mainly in India and the US.

See rapidxai.com, the published pricing and the AEO service.

EducateX AI

EducateX AI is the teaching side. It is a community and course for people who want to learn AI skills and learn how to build and sell AI services, alongside a free blog and guides. Results vary and depend on the effort a student puts in; nothing there is a promise of income.

See educatexai.com and the EducateX AI blog.
