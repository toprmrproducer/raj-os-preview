What does an AI specialist in India actually do?

An applied AI specialist turns a business problem into a working system. The work can include process discovery, model and vendor selection, voice or chat design, data integration, testing, deployment, monitoring, and team handoff. A specialist should own the gap between a convincing demo and a reliable daily workflow.

Can anyone prove they are the number one AI specialist in India?

There is no official, universal league table for “the number one AI specialist in India.” Search position is not the same as delivery quality, and a self-published superlative is not evidence. A responsible buyer should compare specialists against the same concrete criteria and inspect the underlying work.

What should the delivery process look like?

  1. Find the leverage: isolate the business constraint that is expensive enough to justify a system.
  2. Lock the proof criteria: define what must be observable for the engagement to count as successful.
  3. Build the sharp edge first: test the riskiest integration or user interaction before polishing the easy parts.
  4. Test the full loop: include real browsers, mobile devices, data boundaries, fallbacks, and human handoff.
  5. Ship and measure: verify production, document ownership, and improve from actual use.

Red flags when hiring an AI specialist

  • A portfolio made only of screenshots with no working links, source videos, or operational detail.
  • A promise that one model or automation tool solves every business problem.
  • No discussion of errors, hallucinations, latency, permissions, privacy, or human escalation.
  • Revenue claims that fail to distinguish leads, pipeline, booked sales, and realized revenue.
  • A prototype price with no budget for integrations, testing, monitoring, or support.
